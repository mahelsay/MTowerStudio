### Beat T.2 — The red cabin

**INT. MI-8 CABIN — IN FLIGHT OVER NORTHERN SINAI — NIGHT (OCT 6, 1973)**

**Environment:** Total darkness outside the portholes. One red cabin lamp. Engine vibration through every surface.

Commandos sit shoulder to shoulder on the fold-down benches. Faces darkened. RPG-7 launchers upright between knees. Malutka missile cases and mine sacks stacked down the center aisle. A young commando, **PRIVATE MOSTAFA** (19), moves his lips in silent prayer. Across from him, **SERGEANT SAYED** (30s) stares at nothing.

At the forward bulkhead sits **CAPTAIN HAMDI SHALABI** (30s), map case on his knee, eyes closed — not sleeping.

No dialogue. Only rotor thunder and breathing.

**SFX:** Rotor beat, airframe rattle, metal buckles tapping in rhythm.
**MUSIC:** None.

**SUPER:** «سَيْنَاءُ — ٦ أُكْتُوبَرَ ١٩٧٣»

**Assets:** AIR-01 · CMD-01 · CMD-02 · CMD-04 · CMD-06

### Beat T.3 — Phantoms on the radar

**INT. MI-8 COCKPIT / CABIN — IN FLIGHT — NIGHT**

**Environment:** Dim green instrument glow in the cockpit; red lamp in the cabin. Below, the black line of the Mediterranean coast.

The formation hugs the coastal route, then banks right toward Romani. In the lead cockpit, the **SQUADRON LEADER** presses his headset to his ear: a warning over the radio — the radar has picked up a flight of Israeli F-4 Phantoms closing. His jaw sets. He keys the intercom. Shalabi presses the headset to his ear.

**SQUADRON LEADER** *(dramatized, over intercom)*
> يَا حَمْدِي… الرَّادَارْ لَاقِطْ تَشْكِيلْ فَانْتُومْ. هَنِضْطَرّْ نِنَزِّلْ الْقُوَّةْ كُلَّهَا فِي أَقْرَبْ مَنْطِقَةْ.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized, close to the source's words; tight, objecting)*
> يَعْنِي إِيهْ يَا فَنْدِمْ؟ دَهْ هَيْبَعِّدْنِي عَنْ الْكَمِينْ.

**SQUADRON LEADER** *(dramatized, close to the source's words; urgent, protective, no room for argument)*
> لَوْ اِتْضَرَبْنَا وِإِنْتُو جُوَّهْ، هَنِخْسَرْ الطَّيَّارَاتْ وِنِخْسَرْكُو. أَنَا مُهِمِّتِي إِنِّي أَوَصَّلَكْ… وَأَحَافِظْ عَلَى حَيَاتَكْ.

Shalabi looks down the cabin at 20 faces watching him.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; low, controlled — a forced choice, accepted)*
> نَزِّلْنَا.

**SFX:** A radio warning crackling in the headset, intercom hiss, rotor pitch changing as the formation descends.
**MUSIC:** None — tension is carried by the rotors and the radio alone.

**Assets:** AIR-01 · CMD-01 · F-4 Phantom (new asset) · Mi-8 cockpit

### Beat T.4 — The mission *(montage, added v1.2 — 2026-10-18)*

**EXT. SALT MARSH / DESERT ROAD NEAR ROMANI — NIGHT, THEN DAY (OCT 6–7, 1973)**

No dialogue. Fast cuts, each image reused from the episode (Acts Two to Four):

1. Mostafa plunges chest-deep into the black brine, the RPG held high and dry. *(2.6)*
2. A boot tears free of the mud and salt crust. *(2.5)*
3. Shalabi lies flat in the sand, eyes on the wire. *(2.9)*
4. Grains of sand tremble and trickle down a pit wall: the ground is shaking. *(3.4)*
5. The armored brigade fills the road to the horizon. *(3.5)*
6. Sayed's hand closes over Mostafa's trigger hand — not yet. *(3.4)*
7. The lead tank is hit; black smoke pours out. *(3.7)*
8. A line of tanks fires back together. *(4.3)*

**SFX:** The splash of the first shot alone, then each shot's own sound.
**MUSIC:** None through the night shots — only water, mud and breath. On the trembling sand a short, driving drums-and-strings cue slams in, builds, and stops dead.

**SMASH CUT TO BLACK.**
