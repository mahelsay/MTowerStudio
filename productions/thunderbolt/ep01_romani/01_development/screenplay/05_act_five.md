## SEQUENCE A — FIVE DAYS

### Beat 5.1 — West

**EXT. THE AMBUSH AREA — LATE NIGHT**

**Environment:** The glow of the burning column behind them.

The survivors gather in the dark around Shalabi, crouching.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper, every word an order)*
> تَلَاتْ مَجْمُوعَاتْ… كُلّْ مَجْمُوعَةْ لِوَحْدَهَا. وِنِتْقَابِلْ غَرْبْ الْقَنَاةْ.

**MAHMOUD** *(whisper, touching the radio on his back)*
> وِالْجِهَازْ يَا فَنْدِمْ؟

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper)*
> مَعَاكْ… وِمَقْفُولْ.

The survivors split into three groups and slip away from the pits, heading west. Shalabi is the last to leave. He looks back once at the road, then turns and follows his men into the dark.

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-04 · ensemble · ENV-01

### Beat 5.2 — Five days

**EXT. SINAI DUNES — DAYS 1 TO 5**

**Environment:** Each day has a different light: harsh noon, cold blue night, a dawn, a dusk. The dunes are always the same color.

**VO**

> خَمْسَةُ أَيَّامٍ سَيْرًا عَلَى الْأَقْدَامِ… يَحْتَمُونَ بِالْكُثْبَانِ، وَيَمْشُونَ.

A montage of endurance, as close and intimate as the salt march in Act Two:

- Men lying in the shade of a dune crest at midday, not moving.
- Cracked lips. Mahmoud shakes his canteen: empty. Mostafa unscrews his own, looks at the last mouthful in it, and holds it out.

**MOSTAFA** *(a cracked whisper)*
> خُدْ… أَنَا مِشْ عَطْشَانْ.

**MAHMOUD** *(a cracked whisper, not taking it yet)*
> كَدَّابْ.

Mostafa stares at him — Sayed's word. For a moment he cannot speak. Mahmoud takes one sip and gives the canteen back.

- A night march in single file under stars.
- Mostafa, gaunt and sunburned, carrying his rifle and Sayed's.
- Shalabi at the head of the file, checking the compass — the same gesture as Beat 2.3.

**MUSIC:** The march theme from Act Two returns, very softly, as the first day begins.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-03 · C-04 · CMD-02 (gaunt, sunburned state) · ENV-01

### Beat 5.3 — The letter arrives

**EXT./INT. MOSTAFA'S FAMILY HOME, MENOUFIA — DAYS LATER — AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** The same courtyard as Act One, but the oven is cold.

A postman on a bicycle stops at the door and hands Zeinab an envelope. She looks at the handwriting and runs inside.

Umm Mostafa sits on the bench. Zeinab sits beside her and opens the letter — the one Mostafa wrote in the groves before dawn on Oct 6. Her mother's hands are clenched in her lap.

**ZEINAB** *(reading, hopeful, halting)*
> «أُمِّي الْغَالْيَةْ… أَنَا بِخِيرْ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهْ…»

Her voice fades, and **MOSTAFA's voice** carries the letter:

**MOSTAFA** *(V.O., letter — warm and brave; the plea rises)*
> «…مَا تِقْلَقِيشْ عَلَيَّا. عَمّْ سَيِّدْ وِالرِّجَّالَةْ وَاخْدِينْ بَالْهُمْ مِنِّي. سَلِّمِيلِي عَلَى زِينَبْ، وِقُولِيلْهَا تْذَاكِرْ. وِادْعِيلِي يَا أُمِّي… ادْعِيلِي كِتِيرْ.»

Intercut with the dunes: Mostafa, gaunt and sunburned, walking in the file with two rifles on his shoulders.

Zeinab turns the page over and finds the date at the top. Her face changes.

**ZEINAB** *(quiet, frightened, understanding)*
> يَامَّا… دَهْ مَكْتُوبْ يُومْ سِتَّةْ… يُومْ مَا الْحَرْبْ قَامِتْ.

Umm Mostafa takes the letter from Zeinab and presses it flat against her chest. She does not know whether her son is alive. Then she holds it out to Zeinab again.

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(low, steady, refusing to break)*
> اِقْرِيهْ تَانِي.

**MUSIC:** March theme continues softly from Beat 5.2.

**Assets:** C-03 · C-11 · C-12 · ENV-06 · ENV-01 · postman · letter

### Beat 5.4 — Across the water

**EXT. EDGE OF THE TINA PLAIN → THE SUEZ CANAL — DAY 5, DUSK → NIGHT**

**Environment:** The dunes give way to the flat salt marsh where the company landed in Act Two — now seen in fading daylight. Then night on the canal: black water, the far bank a low dark line.

The survivors come down off the last dune and stop at the edge of the salt flat. Mostafa stares at it: the same white crust, the same brine. Across the flat, a figure in Egyptian uniform rises from cover and raises his rifle.

**INFANTRY SENTRY** *(shouting across the flat, rifle up, nervous)*
> مِينْ هِنَاكْ؟!

Shalabi raises both empty hands.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; raised, hoarse, five days of thirst in it)*
> صَاعْقَةْ!

The sentry lowers his rifle — then waves both arms. Egyptian infantry rise from their positions.

**VO**

> فَالْتَقَوْا بِقُوَّاتِ الْمُشَاةِ الْمِصْرِيَّةِ عَلَى طَرَفِ سَهْلِ الطِّينَةِ… وَعَبَرُوا الْقَنَاةَ فِي الْقَوَارِبِ.

Night. Small boats push off from the east bank. The survivors sit low in them, weapons across their knees, as the oars dip without a sound. Someone hands Mostafa a canteen; he drinks, then passes it on before he has finished.

On the west bank, Shalabi steps out of the boat and stands still. He counts his men again, as he did after the minefield in Beat 2.11 — one shoulder at a time. There are far fewer than there were. He says nothing.

**MUSIC:** March theme continues, still restrained.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-03 · Egyptian infantry (one sentry speaks) · small boats · ENV-03 (dusk) · Suez Canal at night (new: ENV-10)

> **Revision note (v1.2, decision D-6):** This beat now blends the podcast's five-day walk with the Egyptian press account: three groups crossed the Tina Plain, met Egyptian infantry, and crossed the canal by boat.

## SEQUENCE B — THE LETTERS

### Beat 5.5 — The son returns

**EXT. VILLAGE LANE → MOSTAFA'S FAMILY HOME — NOVEMBER 1973 — DAY**

**SUPER:** «نُوفَمْبِرُ ١٩٧٣»

**Environment:** The green fields again. Soft winter sun.

Mostafa walks down the lane in a clean uniform, a small bag on his shoulder. He is thinner and older than in Act One. Zeinab, carrying water, sees him first. She drops the jar and runs.

**ZEINAB** *(shouting, running, beside herself)*
> مُصْطَفَى! يَامَّا… مُصْطَفَى!

In the courtyard, Umm Mostafa rises from the oven, flour on her hands. She crosses to him slowly, as if he might disappear. She touches his face with both hands.

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(barely a whisper)*
> مُصْطَفَى…

He bends and kisses her hand. She leaves flour on his cheek. He looks past her at the oven, where a round of pastry is baking, and almost smiles.

**MOSTAFA** *(soft, his voice catching)*
> عَامْلَةْ فِطِيرْ؟

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(laughing and crying at once)*
> عَامْلَاهُولَكْ كُلّْ يُومْ.

**MUSIC:** March theme, gentle.

**Assets:** C-03 · C-11 · C-12 · ENV-06 · CMD-02 (clean, post-war state)

---

### Beat 5.6 — Sayed's letter

**INT. SHUBRA STAIRWELL / FLAT, CAIRO — NOVEMBER 1973 — DAY**

**Environment:** The same flat. The window is still painted blue, but daylight seeps around its edges.

Mostafa stands at the door. Fatma opens it. She sees a young soldier alone, and she understands before he speaks. Ali peers from behind her skirt.

**MOSTAFA** *(grief-stricken duty; he has carried this for days)*
> عَمّْ سَيِّدْ… وَصَّانِي أَدِّيهُولِكْ بِإِيدِي.

He holds out the folded letter — stained with salt and sand. She takes it. She opens it.

**SAYED** *(V.O., letter — calm, loving, at peace)*
> «فَاطْمَةْ… لَوْ الْجَوَابْ دَهْ وِصِلِّكْ مِنْ إِيدْ غِيرْ إِيدِي، يِبْقَى خَلَاصْ. مَا تِعَيَّطِيشْ قُدَّامْ عَلِي. قُولِيلُهْ أَبُوهْ رَاحْ عَشَانْ نِرْجَعْ السُّوِيسْ… وِرَبِّيهْ رَاجِلْ. وِأَنَا مِشْ نَدْمَانْ.»

Fatma folds the letter. She does not cry — not in front of Ali. She puts her hand on her son's head. Ali looks up at Mostafa.

**ALI** *(small, serious)*
> إِنْتَ كُنْتْ مَعَ بَابَا؟

Mostafa kneels to the boy's height.

**MOSTAFA** *(gentle, steady, for the boy)*
> كُنْتْ مَعَاهْ. وِكَانْ بِيِحْكِي عَنَّكْ عَلَى طُولْ.

Fatma steps back from the doorway.

**FATMA** *(quiet, composed, holding everything in)*
> اِتْفَضَّلْ يَا ابْنِي.

Mostafa's eyes go to Sayed's photo on the wall behind them. He straightens, just slightly, the way a soldier does — then steps inside.

**MUSIC:** March theme, carrying into the next beat.

**Assets:** C-03 · C-13 · C-14 · ENV-07 · Sayed's letter · Sayed's photo

## SEQUENCE C — EPILOGUE

### Beat 5.7 — The road

**EXT. THE ROMANI ROAD — DUSK**

**Environment:** Red dusk. Burned-out hulls along the empty road. Thin smoke rising into a violet sky.

A slow, wide, still shot of the road where the ambush happened. Nothing moves.

**VO**

> كَانَ الْمَطْلُوبُ ثَلَاثَ سَاعَاتٍ أَوْ أَرْبَعًا… وَامْتَدَّ أَثَرُ الْكَمِينِ ثَمَانِيًا وَأَرْبَعِينَ سَاعَةً.

**MUSIC:** March theme builds gently — the fullest it has been in the film, but never loud.

**Assets:** ENV-02 · ARM-04 (burned-out state) · ATM-03
