**What changed from v1.0 (drama-first):** the story is now told by the people in it, not by the narrator. The men argue, joke, fear and give orders on camera; the families talk to each other. Narration drops from 6 beats to 4 single lines (1.1, 1.4, 1.9, 1.11) that only set time and place. Every fact the old narration carried (the competitions, 122 men, the Malutka platoon and engineers, four magazines per man, the mission, three or four hours, no body armour) is now said by a character. Dialogue lines: **19 → 78**. Most v1.0 lines are kept; the script board sharpened a few (1.2 lines 1 and 7, 1.4 line 3).

**Conventions:** as in `00_teaser.md`. **Real people** (Shalabi, the Commander) only speak around their documented actions; their lines are marked *(dramatized)*. **Composites** (Sayed, Mostafa, Mahmoud, Hassan, Adel, the families) carry the personal moments. Each line is one or two short sentences, so every line can be a lip-synced close shot; the parenthetical is the performance (how loud, how fast, what is felt).

## SEQUENCE A — THE BEST COMPANY

### Beat 1.1 — Salt and sweat

**EXT. SA'IQA TRAINING AREA, SALT FLATS — SUMMER 1973 — DAY**

**Environment:** Blinding white salt flats under a brutal sun. Heat shimmer. Shallow brine at the ankles.

**SUPER:** «صَيْفُ ١٩٧٣»

**EPISODE TITLE CARD:** «كَمِينُ رُمَّانَةَ» — *The Romani Ambush*

A long file of commandos marches through the brine in full kit, weapons held high. Close: boots white with salt.

**VO**

> قَبْلَ الْحَرْبِ بِشُهُورٍ، كَانَتْ وَحَدَاتُ الصَّاعِقَةِ تُقِيمُ مُسَابَقَاتٍ بَيْنَ سَرَايَاهَا.

**SERGEANT SAYED** (C-02) wades back along the file, driving the men.

**SAYED** *(shouting down the file — loud, rough, pushing them)*
> يَلَّا يَا رِجَّالَةْ! السَّرِيَّةْ التَّانْيَةْ سَابْقَانَا بِدِقِيقْتِينْ!

**PRIVATE ADEL** (C-06), tall and thin, wades beside **PRIVATE MOSTAFA** (C-03).

**ADEL** *(panting, to Mostafa — dry, deadpan)*
> دِقِيقْتِينْ إِيهْ… دَانَا رِجْلَيَّا بِقِتْ مِلْحْ.

Mostafa laughs — and slips. He goes down into the brine. Sayed is there in two strides, hauls him up by the webbing, and shoves the RPG launcher back above his head.

**SAYED** *(close to his face, hard, not shouting)*
> إِنْتَ تِتْبِلّْ… السِّلَاحْ لَأْ. فَاهِمْ؟

**MOSTAFA** *(spitting salt water, ashamed)*
> فَاهِمْ.

At the finish line, marked with flags, **CAPTAIN HAMDI SHALABI** (C-01) stands with a stopwatch in his hand, counting his men across one by one. Most of the company is already in. Sayed arrives beside him, breathing hard.

**SAYED** *(catching his breath, a little proud)*
> أَوِّلْ رَاجِلْ عَدَّى مِنْ بَدْرِي يَا فَنْدِمْ.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; low, eyes still on the last man in the brine)*
> لِسَّهْ مَا وِصِلْنَاشْ.

Mostafa, the last man, stumbles across the line. Only then does Shalabi press the stopwatch.

Later, at a dusty parade ground, the **COMMANDER OF SA'IQA FORCES** (C-07) reads results from a clipboard. Shalabi's men stand rigid. Only their eyes move.

**COMMANDER** *(dramatized; formal, carrying across the parade ground)*
> أَكْفَأْ سَرِيَّةْ… السَّرِيَّةْ الْأُولَى. النَّقِيبْ حَمْدِي شَلَبِي.

In the second rank, **CORPORAL MAHMOUD** (C-04), glasses taped at the hinge, whispers without turning his head.

**MAHMOUD** *(a whisper, half thrilled, half afraid)*
> عَارْفِينْ يَعْنِي إِيهْ الْأُولَى؟

**ADEL** *(whisper, hopeful)*
> يَعْنِي إِجَازَةْ؟

**MAHMOUD** *(whisper)*
> يَعْنِي أَوِّلْ مَا الْحَرْبْ تِقُومْ… أَتْقَلْ مُهِمَّةْ هَتِتْرِمِي عَلِينَا.

**SAYED** *(front rank, not turning his head; low and final)*
> اِسْكُتْ إِنْتَ وِهُوَّ.

They shut up. Adel's mouth twitches.

**SFX:** Wading, heavy breathing, wind; a stopwatch click; the Commander's voice echoing off the barracks walls.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · C-03 · C-04 · C-06 · C-07 · ensemble · ENV-08 (training salt flats) · parade ground · CMD-02 (clean → salt-crusted) · stopwatch

### Beat 1.2 — "Afraid?"

**INT. SA'IQA BARRACKS — SUMMER 1973 — NIGHT**

**Environment:** A long barracks room. Iron bunks. One bare bulb. Kit laid out on blankets.

Sayed crouches beside Mostafa, who is struggling with the straps of his RPG rocket bag. Sayed takes the bag without a word, re-threads the straps, and pulls them tight.

**SAYED** *(patient, low; a teacher, not a sergeant)*
> شُدّْ السُّيُورْ كِدَهْ… عَشَانْ لَمَّا تِجْرِي، الشَّنْطَةْ مَا تْخَبَّطْشْ فِيكْ.

He hands it back. A beat.

**SAYED** *(quiet, looking straight at him)*
> خَايِفْ؟

**MOSTAFA** *(too fast)*
> لَأْ.

**SAYED** *(dry, almost a smile)*
> كَدَّابْ… وَأَنَا كَمَانْ خَايِفْ.

Mostafa laughs despite himself. Then, quieter:

**MOSTAFA** *(curious, careful — he has never asked him anything personal)*
> وِإِنْتَ خَايِفْ مِنْ إِيهْ يَا عَمّْ سَيِّدْ؟

Sayed's eyes go to a small photo tucked into the frame of his bunk: a woman and a little boy in front of the sea.

**SAYED** *(a long beat; low, honest, the only time he says it)*
> إِنِّي مَا أَرَجَّعْشْ عَلِي ابْنِي لِلسُّوِيسْ… زَيّْ مَا وَعَدْتُهْ.

He slaps the back of Mostafa's neck lightly and stands.

**SAYED** *(back to his gruff self, already moving on)*
> نَامْ يَا ابْنِي… طَابُورْ بُكْرَةْ السَّاعَةْ أربعة.

He moves on down the row.

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-02 · C-03 · ENV-09 (barracks) · CMD-04 · CMD-06 · Sayed's family photo (small)

## SEQUENCE B — TWO HOMES

### Beat 1.3 — The letter from the front

**EXT./INT. MOSTAFA'S FAMILY HOME, A VILLAGE IN MENOUFIA — SEPTEMBER 1973 — DAY**

**Environment:** A mud-brick house at the edge of green fields. A clay bread oven in the courtyard. Chickens. Bright Delta light, green and gold — the opposite of the desert.

**UMM MOSTAFA** (C-11), in a black galabeya and headscarf, slaps dough onto the hot oven wall. Her daughter **ZEINAB** (C-12), 12, sits on the step, unfolding a letter carefully. Her mother cannot read; Zeinab reads for her.

**ZEINAB** *(reading slowly, sounding out her brother's handwriting)*
> «أُمِّي الْغَالْيَةْ… أَنَا بِخِيرْ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهْ. التَّدْرِيبْ صَعْبْ…»

Her voice fades out and **MOSTAFA's voice** takes over the letter:

**MOSTAFA** *(V.O., letter — light and brave, for his mother)*
> «…بَسّْ أَنَا قَدُّهْ.»

Umm Mostafa stops working. She smiles at the oven, not at Zeinab.

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(to herself, proud, a murmur)*
> قَدُّهْ…

Zeinab peeks at the next line and grins.

**ZEINAB** *(mischievous, teasing)*
> وِكَاتِبْ كَمَانْ… إِنُّهْ نِفْسُهْ فِي فِطِيرْ مِنْ إِيدِكْ.

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(not turning; her hands slow down)*
> اِقْرِي اللِّي مَكْتُوبْ… مَا تِأَلِّفِيشْ.

**ZEINAB** *(holding up the page, laughing)*
> وَاللَّهِ مَكْتُوبْ!

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(a long beat; to the fire, softly)*
> يِبْقَى أَعْمِلُّهْ… يِمْكِنْ يِيجِي.

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-11 · C-12 · ENV-06 (village home) · letter prop · bread oven

### Beat 1.4 — The orders

**INT. COMMAND TENT, SA'IQA FORCES — OCT 5, 1973 — 7:00 P.M.**

**Environment:** Paraffin lamp. Map boards. Canvas walls breathing in the wind.

Shalabi stands at attention before the **COMMANDER OF SA'IQA FORCES** and an **OPERATIONS AUTHORITY REPRESENTATIVE**. A sealed envelope lies on the field table between them. The representative says nothing all evening; he only watches Shalabi's face.

**VO**

> فِي الْخَامِسِ مِنْ أُكْتُوبَرَ، اسْتُدْعِيَ النَّقِيبُ حَمْدِي شَلَبِي، قَائِدُ السَّرِيَّةِ الْأُولَى مِنَ الْكَتِيبَةِ مِئَةٍ وَثَلَاثَةٍ وَثَمَانِينَ صَاعِقَةْ.

**COMMANDER** *(dramatized; measured, testing him)*
> رِجَّالْتَكْ جَاهْزِينْ؟

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; firm, without hesitation)*
> جَاهْزِينْ يَا فَنْدِمْ.

**COMMANDER** *(dramatized; sliding the envelope across; lower, slower, every word an order)*
> الظَّرْفْ دَهْ فِيهْ تَفَاصِيلْ الْمُهِمَّةْ. مَا تِفْتَحْهُوشْ إِلَّا بِأَمْرْ.
> *(beat)*
> جَاهِزْ يَا حَمْدِي؟

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; taking it; resolute, quiet)*
> جَاهِزْ يَا فَنْدِمْ.

Outside, in the dark, Sayed has been waiting by the tent rope. He falls in beside Shalabi as he walks out.

**SAYED** *(low, searching his face)*
> خِيرْ يَا فَنْدِمْ؟

Shalabi touches the breast pocket where the envelope now is.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; quiet, certain)*
> خَلِّي الرِّجَّالَةْ جَاهْزِينْ يَا سَيِّدْ. جِهْ دُورْنَا.

**SAYED** *(straight to the point)*
> إِمْتَى يَا فَنْدِمْ؟

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; walking on, not slowing)*
> لَمَّا يِيجِي الْأَمْرْ.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · C-07 · sealed envelope prop · 1973 command tent

### Beat 1.5 — The blue windows

**INT. ONE-ROOM FLAT, SHUBRA, CAIRO — OCT 5, 1973 — EVENING**

**SUPER:** «الْقَاهِرَةُ — حَيُّ شُبْرَا»

**Environment:** One small room that is bedroom, kitchen, and workshop at once. The window glass is painted dark blue — the wartime blackout. A single bulb.

**FATMA** (C-13), about 30, works a foot-pedal sewing machine. On the wall above it: a framed photo of Sayed in uniform, and an old postcard of the Suez seafront. A packed suitcase sits on top of the wardrobe, never unpacked since 1967.

Her son **ALI** (C-14), 8, lies on the floor flying a tin toy airplane, making engine noises.

**ALI** *(playing, proud)*
> بَابَا قَالْ هَيِرَكِّبْنِي طَيَّارَةْ بِجَدّْ.

**FATMA** *(not looking up from the needle; a tired, fond smile)*
> بَابَاكْ بِيِوْعِدْ كْتِيرْ.

Ali lands the plane on the floor.

**ALI** *(suddenly serious, the way children are)*
> مَامَا… إِحْنَا هَنِرْجَعْ السُّوِيسْ إِمْتَى؟

The sewing machine stops. Fatma looks at the photo of Sayed.

**FATMA** *(quiet, certain, for the boy's sake)*
> لَمَّا بَابَاكْ يِرَجَّعْهَا.

**ALI** *(he will not let it go)*
> وِهُوَّ هَيِرَجَّعْهَا إِمْتَى؟

Fatma doesn't answer. The machine starts again — louder.

**SFX:** The rhythmic clatter of the sewing machine; street noise muffled through the painted glass.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-13 · C-14 · ENV-07 (Shubra flat) · sewing machine · Sayed's photo · Suez postcard · suitcase · tin airplane

## SEQUENCE C — THE WAITING

### Beat 1.6 — Waiting in the groves

**EXT. TREE GROVES NEAR SALHIYA AIRFIELD — OCT 6, 1973 — 3:00 A.M. → MORNING**

**Environment:** Dark eucalyptus and scrub. Pre-dawn chill. Later, flat morning light through branches.

The company lies dispersed beneath the trees, each man beside his weapon and kit. Close details: AK magazines being checked. F-1 grenades in pouches. Round anti-tank mines stacked under netting. The Malutka platoon checks wire-guided missiles in their suitcase launchers. Engineers coil marking tape onto reels.

Shalabi sits apart, the envelope still sealed in his breast pocket. Mahmoud kneels beside him with the radio and a notebook.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; low)*
> عَدِّيتْهُمْ يَا مَحْمُودْ؟

**MAHMOUD** *(reading from the notebook, precise)*
> مية واتنين وعشرين يَا فَنْدِمْ… بِفَصِيلْةِ الْمَالُوتْكَا وِالْمُهَنْدِسِينْ.

Down the line, Sayed walks the row of resting men.

**SAYED** *(loud enough for the whole row; no nonsense)*
> أَرْبَعْ خَزَايِنْ لِكُلّْ وَاحِدْ. اللِّي نَاقْصُهْ حَاجَةْ يِقُولْ دِلْوَقْتِي… مِشْ هُنَاكْ.

Adel kneels over his Malutka case, turning the wire spool with one finger. Beside him **ENGINEER SERGEANT HASSAN** (C-05) coils white tape onto a reel without looking up.

**ADEL** *(half joking, half not)*
> يَا شَاوِيشْ حَسَنْ… السِّلْكْ دَهْ لَوْ اتْقَطَعْ، الصَّارُوخْ يِرُوحْ فِينْ؟

**HASSAN** *(the slowest, calmest voice in the company; still not looking up)*
> يِرُوحْ أَعْمَى… عَشَانْ كِدَهْ ثَبِّتْ إِيدَكْ.

Adel looks down at his own hands. He flexes them.

Time passes. The light turns from grey to flat white. Mahmoud presses the headset to one ear, then lowers it.

**MAHMOUD** *(quiet, apologetic, as if it were his fault)*
> لِسَّهْ مَافِيشْ إِشَارَةْ يَا فَنْدِمْ.

Shalabi nods. The envelope stays sealed.

**SFX:** Magazines clicking into pouches; a spool ticking; birds; the hiss of an open radio channel.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · C-04 · C-05 · C-06 · CMD-02 · CMD-04 · CMD-05 · CMD-06 · CMD-07 · Malutka/Sagger launcher · radio · notebook

### Beat 1.7 — Letters

**EXT. TREE GROVES NEAR SALHIYA AIRFIELD — OCT 6, 1973 — BEFORE DAWN**

**Environment:** Grey pre-dawn light through the branches. Men asleep or half-asleep against their packs.

Mostafa writes on a page balanced on his rifle stock, with a pencil stub, by the first light.

**MOSTAFA** *(V.O., letter — warm, light, hiding the fear)*
> «أُمِّي الْغَالْيَةْ… أَنَا بِخِيرْ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهْ. مَا تِقْلَقِيشْ عَلَيَّا…»

Adel rolls over against his pack, half awake.

**ADEL** *(sleepy, teasing)*
> بِتِكْتِبْ لِمِينْ يَا مُصْطَفَى؟ لِلْبِتّْ اللِّي فِي الْبَلَدْ؟

**MOSTAFA** *(not looking up)*
> لِأُمِّي.

**ADEL** *(the grin fades; he lies back and closes his eyes)*
> … سَلِّمْلِي عَلِيهَا.

A few meters away, Sayed writes too — slowly, crossing out a word, starting again. He folds his page twice, looks at it a long moment, and holds it out to Mostafa.

**SAYED** *(plain and practical, so the boy will not see what it costs him)*
> خُدْ دَهْ. لَوْ مَا رِجِعْتِشْ… وَصَّلُهْ لِفَاطْمَةْ بِإِيدَكْ.

**MOSTAFA** *(insistent, upset, refusing the thought)*
> إِنْتَ رَاجِعْ يَا عَمّْ سَيِّدْ.

**SAYED** *(gentle, closing the subject)*
> خَلِّيهْ مَعَاكْ وِخَلَاصْ.

Mostafa buttons the letter into his breast pocket. Sayed lies back and closes his eyes.

**SFX:** Birds waking, a cough, the scratch of a pencil.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-02 · C-03 · C-06 · letters (two) · ENV-05

### Beat 1.8 — The tenth of Ramadan

**EXT. VILLAGE, MENOUFIA — OCT 6, 1973 — NOON**

**Environment:** The village at midday in Ramadan: quiet lanes, men dozing in the shade, the smell of baking bread for the evening.

Umm Mostafa and Zeinab carry a tray of loaves home through a lane. An ordinary fasting day. A boy pulls a donkey cart. A radio somewhere plays an old Egyptian song. They stop in the shade of a wall to shift the tray.

**ZEINAB** *(out of nowhere, the way the thought comes)*
> يَامَّا… هُوَّ مُصْطَفَى صَايِمْ هِنَاكْ؟

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(plain)*
> صَايِمْ… وِبِيِتْعَبْ.

**ZEINAB** *(worried, practical)*
> طَبْ هَيِفْطَرْ عَلَى إِيهْ؟

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(a beat; she doesn't know, and won't say so; she lifts the tray again)*
> اللِّي رَبِّنَا يِبْعَتُهْ.

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-11 · C-12 · ENV-06 (village lanes)

### Beat 1.9 — 2:00 P.M.: the war begins

**EXT. TREE GROVES NEAR SALHIYA AIRFIELD — OCT 6, 1973 — 2:00 P.M.**

**Environment:** Hard afternoon sun. Heat shimmer.

A roar tears the sky. Egyptian jets pass low overhead, eastbound toward the canal. Every head in the grove lifts.

**VO**

> فِي السَّاعَةِ الثَّانِيَةِ مِنْ ظُهْرِ السَّادِسِ مِنْ أُكْتُوبَرَ… بَدَأَتِ الْحَرْبُ.

**MOSTAFA** *(on his feet, staring up, shouting over the roar)*
> دُولْ بِتُوعْنَا؟!

**SAYED** *(raised over the roar, his hand on Mostafa's shoulder; his voice catches)*
> أَيْوَهْ… بِتُوعْنَا!

**ADEL** *(barely a whisper)*
> يَعْنِي… خَلَاصْ؟

Nobody answers. A commando grips his neighbour's arm. Nobody cheers. They understand.

Mahmoud runs up through the trees, the headset half off his head.

**MAHMOUD** *(breathless, urgent)*
> يَا فَنْدِمْ! إِشَارَةْ فَتْحْ الظَّرْفْ وِصْلِتْ!

Shalabi breaks the seal and reads. His face gives nothing away. Sayed and the platoon leaders gather in close. Shalabi looks up.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; low, every word placed)*
> كَمِينْ عَلَى طَرِيقْ رُمَّانَةْ – بَالُوظَةْ. لِوَاءْ مُدَرَّعْ نَازِلْ عَلَى الْقَنَاةْ… وِإِحْنَا هَنْعَطَّلُهْ.

**SAYED** *(steady)*
> قَدّْ إِيهْ يَا فَنْدِمْ؟

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; folding the order into his pocket)*
> تلات سَاعَاتْ… أربعة. لَحَدّْ مَا رِجَّالِتْنَا يِثَبِّتُوا رِجْلِيهُمْ شَرْقْ الْقَنَاةْ.

**SFX:** Jet roar fading east; then a silence full of insects.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · C-03 · C-04 · C-06 · CMD-01 · Egyptian Air Force jets (type TBD) · opened orders prop · radio

### Beat 1.10 — "Here is Cairo"

**INT. SHUBRA FLAT / STAIRWELL — OCT 6, 1973 — AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** Same flat. Afternoon light glowing blue through the painted window.

Later that afternoon, a neighbor's radio, turned up loud, echoes up the stairwell with the first communiqué of the crossing.

**RADIO ANNOUNCER** *(MSA, dramatized paraphrase)*
> هُنَا الْقَاهِرَةُ… بَيَانٌ مِنَ الْقِيَادَةِ الْعَامَّةِ لِلْقُوَّاتِ الْمُسَلَّحَةِ: نَجَحَتْ قُوَّاتُنَا فِي عُبُورِ قَنَاةِ السُّوَيْسِ…

From the stairwell, women's ululation — **زَغَارِيد** — rises floor by floor. Fatma's foot stops on the sewing pedal. Ali runs to the window and scratches at the blue paint with his fingernail until a tiny clear spot appears. He presses his eye to it.

**ALI** *(shouting, beside himself)*
> مَامَا! بِيِقُولُوا عَدِّينَا! عَدِّينَا!

**FATMA** *(not moving; very quiet)*

**FATMA** *(a long beat; then, finally, she smiles — and her eyes fill)*

### Beat 1.11 — Six Mi-8s

**EXT. SALHIYA AIRFIELD — OCT 6, 1973 — SUNSET → 6:00 P.M.**

**Environment:** Last light gone. Blue-black sky. Rotor wash whipping dust across the apron.

In the minutes after sunset on the tenth of Ramadan (about 5:35 P.M.), the men break their fast standing beside the aircraft: a date and a mouthful of water from a canteen, passed hand to hand.

**SAYED** *(passing dates down the line, shouting over the rotors)*
> بَلَحَةْ وِبُقّْ مَيَّةْ! الْعَشَا بُكْرَةْ فِي سِينَا!

**ADEL** *(taking one, grinning, raised over the rotors)*
> إِنْ شَاءْ اللَّهْ يِكُونْ فِيهْ عِيشْ هِنَاكْ.

Mostafa holds his date and doesn't eat it. Sayed closes the boy's hand around it.

**SAYED** *(leaning in close to his ear over the noise; gruff and kind)*
> كُلْ. الْخُوفْ مَا بِيْشَبَّعْشْ.

Mahmoud tugs at his web gear and looks along the line of men in plain field uniforms.

**MAHMOUD** *(raised over the rotors; half joking, half not)*
> هُوَّ مَافِيشْ وَلَا حِتِّةْ صَاجْ عَ الصَّدْرْ؟

**SAYED** *(raised, slapping Mahmoud's shoulder, then the shoulder of the man beside him)*
> لَأْ. إِنْتَ غَطِّي ضَهْرُهْ… وِهُوَّ يِغَطِّي ضَهْرَكْ.

Six Mi-8 helicopters, rotors turning. Shalabi stands at the door of the lead aircraft.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; shouting over the rotor roar, an arm sweeping them in)*
> يَلَّا! اِطْلَعُوا! اِطْلَعُوا!

The company runs aboard in sticks: rifles, RPG-7 launchers and rocket bags, Malutka cases, mine sacks, engineer probes; field uniforms and web gear only.

**VO**

> عِنْدَ السَّادِسَةِ مَسَاءً، أَقْلَعَتْ سِتُّ مِرْوَحِيَّاتٍ مِنْ طِرَازِ «مِي ثَمَانِيَة» مِنْ مَطَارِ الصَّالِحِيَّةِ.

The last commando is hauled in. The door slides shut. The formation lifts off.

### Beat 1.12 — Iftar

**EXT./INT. MOSTAFA'S FAMILY HOME — OCT 6, 1973 — SUNSET**

**Environment:** Orange sunset over the fields. The call to prayer from the village mosque.

The family sits around a low round table (طَبْلِيَّة) with bread, beans, and dates. Zeinab breaks her fast with a date. Umm Mostafa has set one more plate than there are people. It stays empty.

**ZEINAB** *(pointing at it, gently)*
> يَامَّا… الطَّبَقْ دَهْ لِمِينْ؟

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(simply)*

**ZEINAB** *(softly, not wanting to hurt her)*
> بَسّْ هُوَّ مِشْ هِنَا.

She goes to the doorway and looks up at the darkening sky to the east.

**EXT. NIGHT SKY OVER SINAI**

Six dark helicopter silhouettes against the last violet light, flying low, east.
