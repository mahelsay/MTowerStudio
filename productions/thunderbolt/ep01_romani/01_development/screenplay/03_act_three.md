## SEQUENCE A — THE TEST

### Beat 3.1 — The reconnaissance flight

**EXT. AMBUSH AREA — OCT 7, 7:00 A.M.**

**Environment:** Clear morning sky. Low sun.

**VO**

> فِي السَّابِعَةِ صَبَاحًا… طَائِرَةُ اسْتِطْلَاعٍ إِسْرَائِيلِيَّةٌ.

A rising engine whine. Sayed, in his pit, hisses to the pits on either side.

**SAYED** *(whisper, sharp)*
> مَحَدِّشْ يِتْحَرَّكْ… وَلَا رِمْشْ.

An Israeli aircraft (AIR-04) appears low over the road, banking slowly, sweeping the desert on both sides. Inside the pits, nobody moves. Close: Mostafa's eye, unblinking, as the aircraft's shadow passes over his pit. The engine fades. A long silence.

**ADEL** *(a whisper from the next pit, barely breathing)*
> شَافِتْنَا؟

**SAYED** *(whisper, dry)*
> لَوْ كَانِتْ شَافِتْنَا… كُنْتْ عِرِفْتْ لِوَحْدَكْ.

**SFX:** One continuous pass: the jet engine growing to a roar, then fading. Silence returns.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** AIR-04 · C-02 · C-03 · C-06 · ENV-04

### Beat 3.2 — "They crossed!"

**EXT. VILLAGE GROCERY, MENOUFIA — OCT 7, 1973 — MORNING**

**Environment:** A small grocery with a radio on the counter. Men crowd around it on benches outside; women stand a little apart.

Umm Mostafa stands at the edge of the crowd with her basket, listening. Zeinab squeezes between the men to the counter, listens, and runs back to her mother, breathless.

**ZEINAB** *(breathless joy)*
> عَدُّوا يَا أُمَّهْ! عَدُّوا الْقَنَاةْ!

Her mother takes her by the shoulders.

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(low; the only question that matters)*
> وِمُصْطَفَى؟

Zeinab has no answer. Behind them, the men around the radio cheer.

**ZEINAB** *(trying, for her mother's sake)*
> أَكِيدْ عَدَّى مَعَاهُمْ يَامَّا…

Umm Mostafa looks past her at the cheering men. She lifts her basket.

**UMM MOSTAFA** *(level, closing it)*
> يَلَّا… عَ الْبِيتْ.

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-11 · C-12 · ENV-06 (village grocery) · period radio

### Beat 3.3 — The bait

**EXT. THE ROMANI ROAD — MORNING**

**Environment:** Bright morning light, rising heat.

A jeep leads five tracked vehicles (ARM-05) along the road at a steady speed, crews scanning the dunes. From inside a pit, Shalabi watches them through a gap between his forearm and the sand. Mahmoud lies beside him.

**MAHMOUD** *(whisper, counting)*
> جِيبْ… وِخَمْسْ مُجَنْزَرَاتْ يَا فَنْدِمْ.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper, his hand flat on the sand)*
> دُولْ طُعْمْ… لَوْ ضَرَبْنَاهُمْ، الطَّيَرَانْ هَيِنْزِلْ عَلِينَا. بَلَّغْ: مَحَدِّشْ يِتْحَرَّكْ.

Mahmoud passes the sign to the next pit — flat hand, pressed down. They pass the first platoon, the second, the third. Nobody moves.

**SFX:** Engines and track clatter passing and fading.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** ARM-05 · C-01 · C-04 · ENV-02

### Beat 3.4 — The battalion passes

**EXT. THE ROMANI ROAD — MORNING**

**Environment:** A dust plume (ATM-01) rising on the horizon, growing.

The ground trembles before anything is visible. Then a full Israeli tank battalion of Centurions (ARM-02) rolls into view and down the road, tank after tank, commanders upright in their hatches. In one hatch, a battalion commander *(Assaf Yagouri per the transcript — unnamed on screen, C-09)* scans the desert through binoculars.

**VO**

> ثُمَّ مَرَّتْ كَتِيبَةُ دَبَّابَاتٍ كَامِلَةٌ… أَمَامَ أَعْيُنِهِمْ.

Shalabi takes the handset from Mahmoud.

**SHALABI** *(into the handset, barely audible — dramatized)*
> مَحَدِّشْ يِشْتِبِكْ. سِيبُوهُمْ يِعَدُّوا.

In a pit near the road, Mostafa's RPG is on his shoulder. A tank fills his sight. His finger tightens on the trigger. A hand closes over his — Sayed, in the next pit an arm's length away, has reached across.

**SAYED** *(whispering)*
> مِشْ دُولْ… اِسْتَنَّى.

**MOSTAFA** *(a whisper through his teeth, shaking)*
> دِي فِي وِشِّي يَا عَمّْ سَيِّدْ!

**SAYED** *(whisper, iron-calm, his hand still on Mostafa's)*
> وِإِنْتَ مِشْ فِي وِشَّهَا. سِيبْهَا تِعَدِّي.

Mostafa's finger relaxes. The tanks roll on and away.

**SFX:** Ground rumble, diesel roar, the squeal of tracks; sand trickling into the pits from the vibration.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** ARM-02 · ATM-01 · C-01 · C-02 · C-03 · C-04 · C-09

### Beat 3.5 — The road looks clear

**EXT. THE ROMANI ROAD — LATE MORNING**

**Environment:** Heat shimmer over the asphalt. A second, much larger dust plume on the horizon.

**ISRAELI RADIO** *(Hebrew, Arabic subtitles — dramatized)*
> הַצִּיר נָקִי. אֵין מַגָּע. הַכּוֹחַ הָעִקָּרִי — קָדִימָה.
>
> **Subtitle:** الطريق خالٍ. لا اشتباك. القوة الرئيسية… تقدّموا.

Long-lens shot: the brigade's main column advancing out of the heat shimmer, tanks and vehicles spaced along the road, stretching back further than the eye can follow. They enter the three-kilometer stretch: past the first platoon's positions, the second, the third.

In Shalabi's pit, Mahmoud's lips are at his ear.

**MAHMOUD** *(whisper, urgent)*
> الرَّتْلْ كُلُّهْ دَخَلْ يَا فَنْدِمْ.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper, eyes on the road)*
> لِسَّهْ… لَمَّا آخِرْ دَبَّابَةْ تِعَدِّي الْعَلَامَةْ.

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** ARM-02 · ARM-03 · ATM-01 · ENV-02 · C-01 · C-04

## SEQUENCE B — THE ORDER

### Beat 3.6 — The order

**EXT. SHALABI'S PIT — LATE MORNING / MIDDAY** *(exact time not stated in the source)*

**Environment:** Hard sun overhead. The column's noise fills everything.

Extreme close-up: Shalabi's eyes, moving along the column from front to rear. The last tank passes the marker. He takes the handset. A breath.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; the release of everything held since dawn)*
> اِضْرَبْ!

No narration.

**SFX:** For one second, the column's roar continues unchanged. Then —
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-04

### Beat 3.7 — First and last

**EXT. THE ROMANI ROAD — CONTINUOUS**

**Environment:** Same.

At the front of the column: Adel, kneeling over his control box, guides a Malutka missile in a long, slow, wobbling arc. His lips move.

**ADEL** *(a whisper, eye to the sight, counting the flight)*
> تَلَاتَةْ… أَرْبَعَةْ… خَمْسَةْ…

It strikes the lead tank, which lurches and stops. At the far end of the column: a second missile hits the last tank. It shudders, smoke pouring from it — but its tracks keep turning. Sayed sees it.

**SAYED** *(shouting down the line)*
> الْأَخْرَانِيَّةْ لِسَّهْ مَاشْيَةْ! آرْ بِي جِي عَلِيهَا!

The column is trapped between its first and last tanks.

**SFX:** The hiss of the missile launch, the thin whine of the wire-guided flight, a heavy impact.
**MUSIC:** None — sound only.

**Assets:** C-02 · C-06 · CMD-10 · ARM-02 · ARM-04 (hit-mobile and disabled states)

### Beat 3.8 — The battle

**EXT. THE AMBUSH ZONE — MIDDAY → AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** Smoke rising in columns along 3 km of road: black oily smoke from burning armor, grey-white from rocket strikes (ATM-03). Sun turning amber through the smoke.

A compressed montage of hours, always from the commandos' level:

- RPG teams rising out of pits, firing, dropping back in. Backblast kicking up sand.

**SAYED** *(shouting to his platoon over the guns)*
> اِطْلَعْ… اِضْرَبْ… وِانْزِلْ! مَحَدِّشْ يِقَفْ بَرَّهْ!

- Mostafa feeding a rocket into the launcher for his gunner, hands steady now.

**MOSTAFA** *(shouting, slapping the gunner's helmet — the signal)*
> جَاهِزْ! اِضْرَبْ!

- Adel's second and third missiles striking tanks that try to turn off the road.
- Turrets traversing toward the desert, firing at positions they cannot see; sand erupting behind the pits.
- Hatches opening on burning tanks; crews escaping into smoke. *(No graphic injury shown.)*
- Shalabi moving between pits, crouched low, pointing new targets *(dramatized)*.

**VO**

> وَبِحَسَبِ الْمَصَادِرِ الْمِصْرِيَّةِ، أَصَابَ الْكَمِينُ مَا بَيْنَ خَمْسَ عَشْرَةَ وَثَمَانِيَ عَشْرَةَ دَبَّابَةً.

**SFX:** Layered battle: RPG launches, impacts, tank main guns, machine guns, secondary explosions.
**MUSIC:** None — sound only.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · C-03 · C-06 · CMD-06 · CMD-10 · ARM-02 · ARM-04 · ATM-03

### Beat 3.9 — The hours

**EXT. THE AMBUSH ZONE — AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** The sun a dull disc behind smoke. Sand drifting across the pits after every impact.

The battle settles into a grinding rhythm. Close and personal:

- Adel, eye pressed to the periscope sight, thumb on the control stick, guiding a missile across a kilometer of desert. Impact. He is already reaching for the next missile.
- An RPG team leaves its pit at a crouching run, drops into a new one thirty meters away. Seconds later, a tank shell hits the pit they left.
- Shalabi and Mahmoud running bent double from one platoon to the next, Mahmoud's antenna whipping behind him. Shalabi drops beside a pit and points.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; shouting over the guns, pointing)*
> اللِّي عَ الشِّمَالْ بِتْلِفّْ! اِضْرَبُوهَا قَبْلْ مَا تِطْلَعْ مِنْ الطَّرِيقْ!

- One pit along the line has gone quiet. Shalabi looks at it for a long moment. *(Nothing inside the pit is shown.)* Mahmoud sees his face.

**MAHMOUD** *(raised over the noise, hesitant)*
> يَا فَنْدِمْ…

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; low, already moving)*
> يَلَّا.

He keeps moving.

**SFX:** Battle sound, but with gaps: the whine of the missile wire, Adel's breathing, Shalabi's footsteps.
**MUSIC:** None — sound only.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-04 · C-06 · CMD-06 · CMD-10 · ATM-03 · ENV-04

### Beat 3.10 — War games

**EXT. ALLEY, SHUBRA, CAIRO — OCT 7, 1973 — AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** A narrow alley in hard afternoon light. Laundry lines overhead.

Ali and three other boys play war in the dust: two are "tanks" crawling on all fours, the others are "commandos" jumping out from behind a doorway, making explosion noises. Ali throws a pretend grenade.

**ALI** *(shouting, thrilled)*
> أَنَا الصَّاعْقَةْ! بُوووومْ!

Fatma appears at the building door and calls him sharply.

**FATMA** *(sharp, frightened, too loud)*
> عَلِي! تَعَالَ هِنَا!

He comes, confused. She kneels and holds him tight, longer than he understands.

**ALI** *(muffled against her shoulder, puzzled)*
> إِحْنَا بِنِلْعَبْ يَا مَامَا…

**FATMA** *(eyes shut, holding on)*
> عَارْفَةْ… عَارْفَةْ.

**SFX:** Children's explosion noises — an echo of the real battle in the previous beat.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-13 · C-14 · ENV-07 (alley) · neighborhood children

### Beat 3.12 — Stuck in the sand

**EXT. THE ROMANI ROAD AND DUNES — AFTERNOON** *(Israeli radio voices over the images)*

**Environment:** Deep, soft dunes on both sides of the road. Smoke drifting.

A tank tries to climb off the road toward the pits. Its tracks spin and dig into the soft sand; it slews sideways and stops, engine roaring. Another tank behind it halts, turret swinging, searching.

**ISRAELI TANK COMMANDER** *(over radio, Hebrew — dramatized)*
> מֵאֵיפֹה הֵם יוֹרִים? אֲנִי לֹא רוֹאֶה אוֹתָם!
>
> **Subtitle:** من أين يطلقون النار؟ لا أراهم!

**ISRAELI BRIGADE COMMANDER** *(over radio to his headquarters, Hebrew — dramatized)*
> הַטַּנְקִים נִתְקָעִים בַּחוֹל. תִּשְׁלְחוּ לִי חִי״ר!
>
> **Subtitle:** الدبابات تغرز في الرمل. أرسلوا لي مشاة!

**SFX:** Tracks grinding and spinning in sand; engines straining; radio static.
**MUSIC:** None — sound only.

**Assets:** C-10 · ARM-02 · ARM-04 · ATM-03 · ENV-01

> **Revision note (v1.1):** This beat previously showed the commander of the battalion that had passed (Assaf Yagouri, per the podcast) refusing a recall order. Israeli-sourced accounts say the opposite — that he wanted to turn back and attack the ambush. Because he is a real, named person, the beat now shows only what both accounts agree on: the tanks struggled in the deep sand and the brigade asked for infantry. See `../research/sources.md`.

### Beat 3.13 — Half-tracks behind

**EXT. DESERT BEHIND THE AMBUSH LINE — AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** Behind the platoons, open sand. The battle's smoke on the horizon.

Hassan's rear-security group lies in a shallow fold of sand, facing away from the battle. Hassan hears it first: engines and the clatter of tracks, coming from the wrong direction. He lifts his head. Out of the drifting smoke, a line of Israeli half-tracks (ARM-07) swings off the road behind the ambush line. Infantry jump down from them and spread into a line.

**VO**

> فَدَفَعَ الْعَدُوُّ مُشَاةً عَلَى عَرَبَاتٍ نِصْفِ مُجَنْزَرَةٍ… لِتَلْتَفَّ خَلْفَ الْكَمِينِ.

Hassan looks at his handful of men. He checks his magazine.

**HASSAN** *(a whisper, slow, the calmest voice in the desert)*
> جُمْ مِنْ وَرَا… زَيّْ مَا قُلْنَا. مَحَدِّشْ يِضْرَبْ قَبْلِي.

He gives a single nod.

**MUSIC:** Score enters for the first time since the order to fire — a single low, sustained cue that carries across the act break into Act Four.

**Assets:** C-05 · ARM-07 (half-tracks) · Israeli infantry · ATM-03 · ENV-04

> **Revision note (v1.2, decision D-5):** The podcast says the Israeli infantry landed by helicopter. Israeli sources say two teams of the Shaked unit arrived on eight half-tracks by road, and Egyptian press does not confirm helicopters, so this beat now shows half-tracks.
