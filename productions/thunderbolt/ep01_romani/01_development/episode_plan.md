## 1. Logline

### العربية

> فِي أُكْتُوبَرَ ١٩٧٣، يَهْبِطُ النَّقِيبُ حَمْدِي شَلَبِي وَمِئَةٌ وَاثْنَانِ وَعِشْرُونَ مِنْ رِجَالِ الصَّاعِقَةِ لَيْلًا فِي سَهْلِ الطِّينَةِ، وَيَزْحَفُونَ اثْنَيْنِ وَأَرْبَعِينَ كِيلُومِتْرًا عَبْرَ الْمَلَّاحَاتِ وَالْكُثْبَانِ وَحُقُولِ الْأَلْغَامِ، لِيَنْصِبُوا كَمِينًا لِلِوَاءٍ مُدَرَّعٍ إِسْرَائِيلِيٍّ عَلَى طَرِيقِ رُمَّانَةَ. كَانَ الْأَمْرُ أَنْ يُعَطِّلُوهُ ثَلَاثَ سَاعَاتٍ… فَامْتَدَّ أَثَرُ الْكَمِينِ ثَمَانِيًا وَأَرْبَعِينَ سَاعَةً. وَفِي الْبُيُوتِ، تَنْتَظِرُ عَائِلَاتُهُمْ خَبَرًا لَا يَأْتِي.

### English

> October 1973: forced down early by Israeli Phantoms, Captain Hamdi Shalabi and 122 Egyptian Sa'iqa commandos land in the Tina salt marsh and march 42 km through mud, dunes, and minefields to ambush an Israeli armored brigade on the Romani road. Their orders: delay it for three hours. The ambush's effect lasted forty-eight. At home, their families wait for news that does not come.

**Dramatic engine:** infantry versus armor, silence versus steel, an order for three hours that became forty-eight — and two families who live through the same days without knowing.

## 2. Episode Structure

> **Teaser and acts:** a TV episode is divided into a short **teaser** (cold open) and several **acts**. Each act ends on a strong moment — the **act-out** — that pulls the viewer into the next act.

\* A "clip" is one generated video shot of about 5–10 seconds. Estimates assume about 8 clips per minute; quiet passages use fewer, longer clips and the battle uses more, shorter ones. Final counts are set in the Phase 2 shot lists.

**Narration pacing:** the narrator reads Arabic at about 100–110 words per minute including pauses. Narration should fill no more than about half of any act, leaving room for silence, sound, dialogue, and letters. **Current measure:** ≈ 985 narration words ≈ 9.5 minutes across the episode — from 7% (Act Five, carried by the letters) to 41% (Act Two, the march).

## 3. Historical Fact Lock (Required Before Script Lock)

**Status key:** 📜 = based on the transcript — **accepted as the working source of record** until external verification is complete (roadmap task 1.7) · ⚠️ = known conflict or doubt; the screenplays avoid committing to a specific detail until resolved · ✅ = verified with an external source

External sources and verification results are recorded in `research/sources.md` (roadmap task 1.7).

**Working rules while verification is deferred:**
- Screenplays may use 📜 facts, phrased as the transcript states them.
- For ⚠️ items, screenplays use neutral wording (for example "an Israeli armored brigade", "Israeli tanks") and avoid unit numbers, tank model names in narration, and contested figures.
- Before public release, every 📜 and ⚠️ item must be verified or the episode must carry an on-screen note that it is based on the account of a former Sa'iqa officer.
