## SEQUENCE A — THE LAST STAND

### Beat 4.1 — The rear guard

**EXT. DESERT BEHIND THE AMBUSH LINE — OCT 7, AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** Open sand behind the platoons. Smoke from the burning column rises on the horizon behind the fighters.

Continuous from Act Three. Hassan's rear-security group, a handful of men, lies flat as the line of Israeli infantry advances from the half-tracks. Closer. Closer. Hassan raises his rifle — and for the first time in the film, he shouts.

**HASSAN** *(a full-throated shout, everything he has held in)*
> دِلْوَقْتِي! اِضْرَبْ!

Seen at a distance and at ground level: muzzle flashes, sand kicking up, men bounding forward and dropping.

The firing thins. Then stops.

Close: the reel of white marking tape that Hassan carried through the minefield in Act Two, lying alone in the sand at the edge of an empty position. The wind lifts a loose end of tape.

**VO**

> خَلْفَ الْكَمِينِ، تَصَدَّتِ الْجَمَاعَاتُ الصَّغِيرَةُ لِلْمُشَاةِ الَّذِينَ الْتَفُّوا عَلَيْهِمْ… لَكِنَّ كَثِيرِينَ مِنْ رِجَالِهَا لَمْ يَعُودُوا.

**SFX:** Rifle fire, distant shouting, then wind.
**MUSIC:** The low cue from Beat 3.13 continues and fades out as the tape reel appears.

**Assets:** C-05 · CMD-12 (tape reel) · ARM-07 (half-tracks) · Israeli infantry · ENV-04

### Beat 4.2 — A square missing one side

**EXT. THE AMBUSH ZONE — AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** Amber, smoke-filtered sun. Burning hulls along the road.

At the rear of the column, the last tank — hit by the Malutka in Beat 3.7 but never stopped — grinds forward and swings off the road, opening space. The surviving tanks follow. A high aerial view shows them spreading into a U-shape: a square with one side missing, its open side facing the desert, every gun turning toward the line of pits.

**VO**

> فَانْتَشَرَتِ الدَّبَّابَاتُ عَلَى شَكْلِ مُرَبَّعٍ يَنْقُصُهُ ضِلْعٌ… وَوَجَّهَتْ مَدَافِعَهَا نَحْوَ الْحُفَرِ.

In Shalabi's pit, Mahmoud lifts his head over the lip and sees the turrets swinging toward them.

**MAHMOUD** *(raised, urgent)*
> يَا فَنْدِمْ! بِيْلِفُّوا الْمَدَافِعْ عَلِينَا!

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; shouting down the line)*
> كُلُّهْ يِنْزِلْ فِي حُفْرِتُهْ! يَلَّا!

**SFX:** Engines straining in sand, turrets whining as they traverse.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** ARM-02 · ARM-04 (hit-mobile state) · ATM-03 · ENV-04 · C-01 · C-04

### Beat 4.3 — Forty guns

**EXT. THE PITS — AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** The line of barrel pits under fire. The sun disappears behind smoke and dust.

**VO**

> أَرْبَعُونَ أَوْ خَمْسُونَ دَبَّابَةً… تَضْرِبُ رِجَالًا فِي حُفَرٍ.

The tanks fire together. Sand erupts along the line of pits in walls of dust. Inside his pit, Mostafa presses himself against the wall as sand pours down on him. Beside him, Sayed shields the RPG's optic with his body and shouts into Mostafa's ear between the blasts.

**SAYED** *(shouting over the barrage)*
> اِلْزَقْ فِي الْحِيطَةْ! وِافْتَحْ بُقَّكْ… عَشَانْ وِدَانَكْ!

**MOSTAFA** *(shouting, terrified, sand in his mouth)*
> هِيَّ مِشْ هَتِخْلَصْ؟!

**SAYED** *(shouting, steady as a rock)*
> هَتِخْلَصْ! كُلّْ حَاجَةْ بِتِخْلَصْ!

The camera stays low, inside the pit, looking up at a sky full of dust.

**SFX:** Tank main guns firing in near-unison; impacts that shake the frame; sand hissing down; muffled hearing.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-02 · C-03 · ARM-02 · ATM-01 · ATM-03

### Beat 4.4 — Empty hands

**EXT. THE PITS — LATE AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** Dust settling briefly between salvoes.

Close-ups of things running out:

- Mostafa reaching into the rocket bag. His hand finds nothing. He looks at Sayed.

**MOSTAFA** *(low, stunned)*
> الشَّنْطَةْ فَاضْيَةْ يَا شَاوِيشْ.

- Adel's control box: he guides a missile to its target. Impact. He looks at the empty launcher beside him.

**ADEL** *(to himself, quiet, almost a laugh)*
> دَهْ كَانْ آخِرْ وَاحِدْ.

- A commando turning an AK magazine over, empty.

Adel reaches for a stick-shaped anti-tank grenade (CMD-11).

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-02 · C-03 · C-06 · CMD-06 · CMD-10 · CMD-11

### Beat 4.5 — The run

**EXT. THE PITS → THE TANKS — LATE AFTERNOON**

**Environment:** Smoke drifting between the pits and the nearest tanks, a few dozen meters away.

**VO**

> وَالْقُنْبُلَةُ الْمُضَادَّةُ لِلدَّبَّابَاتِ لَا تُرْمَى إِلَّا مِنْ قَرِيبٍ… فَخَرَجَ الرِّجَالُ مِنْ حُفَرِهِمْ.

Sayed takes an anti-tank grenade. He puts his hand on Mostafa's shoulder — the same gesture as Beat 3.4, when he told him to wait. Then he taps Mostafa's breast pocket, where the letter is.

**MOSTAFA** *(low, pleading, already knowing)*
> عَمّْ سَيِّدْ… خُدْنِي مَعَاكْ.

**SAYED** *(quiet, plain, the last thing he says)*
> لَأْ. إِنْتَ مَعَاكْ حَاجَةْ لَازِمْ تِوَصَّلْهَا.

He climbs out.

Along the line, officers, NCOs, and soldiers rise from their pits and run toward the tanks through the smoke, grenades in hand. Adel among them.

We stay with Mostafa in the pit. He watches Sayed's back disappear into the smoke. An explosion, muffled, somewhere ahead. The smoke drifts. Sayed does not come back.

**SFX:** Running footsteps in sand; tank machine guns; a distant grenade blast; then only Mostafa's breathing.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-02 · C-03 · C-06 · CMD-11 · ARM-02 · ATM-03

### Beat 4.6 — The letter in his pocket

**EXT. MOSTAFA'S PIT — LATE AFTERNOON — CONTINUOUS**

**Environment:** Smoke thinning. The guns have slowed to single shots.

Mostafa sits at the bottom of his pit, back against the sand wall, knees drawn up. His hand goes to his breast pocket. He takes out Sayed's folded letter — damp from the salt march, gritty with sand. He doesn't open it. He holds it against his chest with both hands and closes his eyes.

Then he buttons it back into his pocket, picks up his rifle, and rises to look over the lip of the pit.

No dialogue. No narration.

**SFX:** Distant single tank shots; wind; Mostafa's breathing slowing down.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-03 · Sayed's letter (salt- and sand-stained state)

---

### Beat 4.7 — Nightfall

**EXT. THE AMBUSH ZONE — NIGHT (OCT 7)**

**Environment:** Darkness. Burning hulls along the road glow orange and throw long, flickering shadows across the sand.

The guns have stopped. The tanks hold their positions, engines idling. Nobody approaches the pits. In the firelight, Shalabi raises his head above the lip of his pit and looks along the line. Silence. Mahmoud crouches beside him.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; a whisper, the hardest order of the day)*
> عِدّْ اللِّي فَاضْلِينْ يَا مَحْمُودْ.

Mahmoud looks at him. In Act One he counted a hundred and twenty-two.

**MAHMOUD** *(whisper)*
> حَاضِرْ يَا فَنْدِمْ.

He crawls off into the dark. The number is never said aloud.

**SFX:** Fire crackling, idling diesel engines far off, wind.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-04 · ARM-04 (burning state) · ENV-04

---

### Beat 4.8 — Blackout

**INT. SHUBRA FLAT, CAIRO — NIGHT (OCT 7)**

**Environment:** The city's power is off for the blackout. Total darkness, then the small flame of a kerosene lamp.

Fatma lights the lamp. Its glow finds Ali sitting up on the mattress, wide awake.

**ALI** *(small, the question he has been holding)*
> هُوَّ بَابَا هَيِيجِي إِمْتَى؟

Fatma turns the lamp wick down a little. She sits beside him and pulls the blanket over both of them.

**FATMA** *(gentle, not answering)*
> نَامْ يَا عَلِي.

**ALI** *(he notices she is not lying down)*
> وِإِنْتِي مِشْ هَتْنَامِي؟

**FATMA** *(a tired smile he cannot see)*
> هَنَامْ… لَمَّا تْنَامْ إِنْتَ.

She stays awake, watching the flame.

**SFX:** The scratch of a match; a distant air-raid siren, then silence.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-13 · C-14 · ENV-07 (blackout night) · kerosene lamp

---

### Beat 4.9 — The burial

**EXT. THE PITS — NIGHT**

**Environment:** Moonlight and the distant orange glow of the burning column.

Shalabi moves along the line in the dark with the survivors. They dig graves in the sand and lay their comrades in them. No faces are shown — only hands smoothing the sand, and the silhouettes of kneeling men.

Shalabi kneels at the last grave and smooths the sand with his palm.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; a whisper, his voice nearly gone)*
> اللَّهْ يِرْحَمْكُمْ يَا رِجَّالَةْ… كُنْتُوا رِجَّالَةْ.

Shalabi gathers the weapons of the fallen. Mostafa takes Sayed's rifle and slings it beside his own. Mahmoud stands with the silent radio on his back.

No narration.

**MUSIC:** None — silence. Only the wind and the distant fire.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-03 · C-04 · CMD-05 · ENV-04

**END OF ACT FOUR**
