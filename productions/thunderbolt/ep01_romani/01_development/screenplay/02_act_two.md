**What changed from v1.0 (drama-first):** behind enemy lines the men talk in whispers — orders, fear, jokes and care pass down the file, so the march is lived by people, not described by a narrator. Narration drops from 13 beats to 5 single lines (2.1, 2.2, 2.5, 2.12, 2.15) — places, the five returning helicopters, the distance, and the three hours won before a shot was fired. Every other fact (42 km, the intercepted signal, radio silence and the new route, the wire and the minefield, the flat ground, the three platoons a kilometre apart with rear groups, the barrel pits) is now said by a character. Dialogue lines: **10 → 48**.

**Conventions:** as in `00_teaser.md`. Real people (Shalabi) speak only around documented actions, marked *(dramatized)*. Behind enemy lines every line is whispered or low unless the rotors cover it. The salt march (Beats 2.5–2.6) is the visual centerpiece of the act.

## SEQUENCE A — THE INSERTION

### Beat 2.1 — The low hover

**EXT. TINA PLAIN (سَهْلُ الطِّينَةِ) — NIGHT**

**Environment:** Flat, black, waterlogged salt marsh to every horizon. A thin salt crust over sucking mud and shallow brine. Moonlight on standing water.

**VO**

> سَهْلُ الطِّينَةِ… أَرْضٌ مِنْ طِينٍ وَمِلْحٍ وَمَاءٍ، لَا تَحْتَمِلُ هُبُوطَ طَائِرَةٍ.

The Mi-8s cannot land — the ground would swallow their wheels. The pilots bring them down to a hover two to two-and-a-half meters above the surface. Rotor wash flattens the water into rings and throws brine spray.

At the open door of the lead helicopter, Sayed slaps each man out into the dark. Mostafa reaches the door, looks down at the black water — and freezes.

**SAYED** *(shouting into his ear over the rotors)*
> مِتْرِينْ يَا مُصْطَفَى! اِنْطّْ!

Mostafa jumps. Commandos land shin-deep in mud and brine — up to the knee in a few deeper pockets, never higher. They turn immediately and receive weapons passed down hand to hand: launchers, missile cases, mine sacks.

**SAYED** *(raised, rhythmic, passing gear down from the door)*
> خُدْ! وَرَاكْ! خُدْ!

Adel lands beside Mostafa, sinks to the shin, and pulls one boot up with a wet suck.

**ADEL** *(raised over the rotors, disgusted, deadpan)*
> دِي مِشْ أَرْضْ… دِي شُورْبَةْ!

**SFX:** Rotor wash, splash and suck of bodies hitting mud, grunts, equipment clanking.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** AIR-01 · ENV-03 · C-02 · C-03 · C-06 · CMD-02 · CMD-06 · CMD-07

### Beat 2.2 — The price in the sky

**EXT. TINA PLAIN — NIGHT — CONTINUOUS**

**Environment:** Same. Everything is seen from the mud — the camera never leaves the ground.

The empty helicopters climb away to draw the threat off. Their dark shapes and exhaust glow shrink into the night. The commandos stand in the brine, weapons held above their shoulders, faces turned up.

Far off and small on the horizon: a streak of light, then a distant flash. A soundless orange bloom — one Mi-8 is gone. Seconds later, the dull thud of the explosion rolls across the water.

**MOSTAFA** *(a whisper, horrified, not yet believing it)*
> دِي… دِي وَاحْدَةْ مِنْ بِتُوعْنَا؟

Nobody answers. The glow fades from the wet plain and from the upturned faces. Shalabi does not look away.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; very low, almost to himself)*
> لَوْ كُنَّا لِسَّهْ فُوقْ…

**MAHMOUD** *(whisper)*
> اللَّهْ يِرْحَمْهُمْ.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; low, a debt he will carry)*
> قَائِدْ السِّرْبْ… هُوَّ اللِّي نَجَّانَا.

The remaining rotor sound fades west, toward the canal. Silence.

**VO**

> وَعَادَتِ الْمِرْوَحِيَّاتُ الْخَمْسُ الْبَاقِيَةُ سَالِمَةً إِلَى غَرْبِ الْقَنَاةِ.

**SFX:** Receding rotors; a delayed, distant explosion; then wind and water only.
**MUSIC:** Score enters — a single low, sustained cue under the flash, fading out with the rotors.

**Assets:** AIR-01 (intact + destroyed state) · F-4 Phantom · ENV-03 · C-01 · C-03 · C-04

## SEQUENCE B — THE 42 KM MARCH

### Beat 2.3 — Compass and map

**EXT. TINA PLAIN — NIGHT**

**Environment:** Absolute quiet. Wind across the water. Stars.

Shalabi stands in the brine, map folded small, a red-filtered flashlight cupped in his palm. Compass bearing. He walks a pair of dividers across the map. Measures twice. Sayed wades up beside him.

**SAYED** *(whisper)*
> بِعِيدْ يَا فَنْدِمْ؟

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper, flat)*
> اتنين وأربعين كِيلُو… عَلَى رِجْلِينَا.

A few men back, Adel leans toward Mostafa.

**ADEL** *(whisper, appalled)*
> اتنين وأربعين… مَشْي؟

**MOSTAFA** *(whisper, looking down at the water to his shins)*
> لَأْ… عُومْ.

Adel snorts and covers his mouth. Shalabi folds the map and turns to the men nearest him; down the file, the order passes on in whispers, man to man.

**SHALABI** *(low, to his men — dramatized)*
> السِّلَاحْ فُوقْ رَاسْكُو. مَحَدِّشْ يِغَرَّقْ سِلَاحُهْ.
> *(beat)*
> هَنِمْشِي عَلَى الْبُوصْلَةْ… يَلَّا.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · C-03 · C-06 · compass/map/dividers props · ENV-03

### Beat 2.4 — The intercepted signal

**EXT. TINA PLAIN — NIGHT — MOMENTS LATER**

**Environment:** Same. The company has taken its first steps into the marsh.

The radio operator, **CORPORAL MAHMOUD**, wades beside Shalabi, the set strapped high on his back, handset pressed to his ear. He has just sent a short coded report: the company is on its way to the objective. A pause. The reply comes back in code. He decodes it by red light and looks up.

**MAHMOUD** *(whispering — dramatized)*
> يَا فَنْدِمْ… الْقِيَادَةْ رَدِّتْ. الْعَدُوّْ لَقَطْ إِشَارِتْنَا.

Shalabi takes this in. The enemy has heard them — and may be waiting.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; low, instant)*
> اِقْفِلْ الْجِهَازْ. مَحَدِّشْ يِفْتَحْ لَاسِلْكِي لِحَدّْ مَا أَقُولْ.

Mahmoud's thumb hovers on the switch.

**MAHMOUD** *(whisper, uneasy)*
> كِدَهْ الْقِيَادَةْ مِشْ هَتِعْرَفْ إِحْنَا فِينْ يَا فَنْدِمْ.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper)*
> وَلَا هُمَّ.

The set clicks off. Shalabi taps the dead radio set, then draws a finger across his lips; the sign passes back down the file, man to man — no radios. Sayed has heard.

**SAYED** *(whisper)*
> يِبْقَى مِسْتَنِّيِّينَّا يَا فَنْدِمْ؟

Shalabi raises the compass again and sets a new bearing.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper, pointing into the dark)*
> مِسْتَنِّيِّينَّا عَ السِّكَّةْ اللِّي عَارْفِينْهَا. هَنْلِفّْ… وِنِطْلَعْلُهُمْ مِنْ حِتَّةْ تَانْيَةْ.

The file turns and follows him on the new bearing.

**SFX:** Brief radio squelch, then the click of the set going dead. Wind and water only.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · C-04 · 1970s Soviet-pattern manpack radio · ENV-03

### Beat 2.5 — Twenty kilometers of salt

**EXT. TINA PLAIN — NIGHT — PROGRESSIVE**

**Environment:** Endless wet flat. Salt crust cracking underfoot. Water at the knees, a little above in places — never deeper. This is the visual centerpiece of Act Two.

A long single file of silhouettes against a starlit horizon, every weapon raised overhead.

**VO**

> عِشْرُونَ كِيلُومِتْرًا فِي الْمَاءِ الْمَالِحِ وَالطِّينِ… وَالسِّلَاحُ فَوْقَ الرُّؤُوسِ.

Then the camera moves in and stays close:

- **Boots** tearing free of the mud with a wet suck, salt crust breaking around the ankle.
- **Hands** white with dried salt, knuckles split, gripping an RPG tube above the waterline.
- **Two men** of the Malutka team — **PRIVATE ADEL** and his partner — carrying a missile case between them, forearms shaking, never lowering it.

**ADEL** *(through his teeth, to his partner)*
> مَا تِنَزِّلْهَاشْ… لَوْ شِرْبِتْ مَيَّةْ، نِبْقَى شَايْلِينْ حَدِيدَةْ عَ الْفَاضِي.

- **A face** — Mostafa — salt crystals in his eyebrows, lips cracked, eyes fixed on the back in front of him. His lips move.

**MOSTAFA** *(a whisper to himself, counting his steps to stay sane)*
> تِسْعَةْ وِتِسْعِينْ… مِيَّةْ… وَاحِدْ… اتْنِينْ…

- **A trouser leg** stiffening as salt dries into it, fabric turning grey and rigid.
- **Sayed** moving back along the file, leaning close to each man in turn, touching each raised weapon as he passes.

**SAYED** *(whisper, close to each man as he passes)*
> فُوقْ… السِّلَاحْ فُوقْ. اللِّي إِيدُهْ تِتْعَبْ يِقُولِّي… مِشْ يِقُولْ لِلْمَيَّةْ.

- **Shalabi** glancing back down the file, counting silhouettes, then turning forward again.

**SFX:** Wading, breathing, salt crust cracking, equipment straps creaking.
**MUSIC:** The march theme enters — slow, restrained, built on a low repeating figure. It must never overpower the breathing.

**Assets:** ENV-03 · C-01 · C-02 · C-03 · C-06 · CMD-02 (wet + salt-crusted state) · CMD-06 · CMD-07

### Beat 2.6 — Mostafa goes under

**EXT. TINA PLAIN — NIGHT — PROGRESSIVE**

**Environment:** Same. Shin-deep brine over soft, sucking mud.

Mostafa's boot sticks fast in the mud. He pitches forward onto his knees and goes down into the brine to his chest — but his arms stay straight up, the RPG launcher held clear of the water above his head.

A hand grabs the back of his chest rig and hauls him upright. Sayed. He looks at the launcher — dry — and then at Mostafa.

**MOSTAFA** *(choking, spitting brine, a whisper — proud and shaking)*
> اِتْبَلِّيتْ… بَسّْ السِّلَاحْ لَأْ.

**SAYED** *(whispering — the closest he comes to a smile)*
> شَاطِرْ.

They wade on. Mostafa's face is streaming with salt water. He doesn't wipe it.

**SFX:** A splash, choking, then wading resumes.
**MUSIC:** March theme continues from Beat 2.5.

**Assets:** C-02 · C-03 · CMD-06 · ENV-03

### Beat 2.7 — The first night

**INT. SHUBRA FLAT, CAIRO — NIGHT (OCT 6)**

**Environment:** Darkness. The blue-painted window is a dull rectangle. A neighbor's radio murmurs through the wall.

Ali lies on the mattress with the tin airplane in his fist, eyes open. Fatma sits on the floor beside the sewing machine, knees drawn up, listening to the radio through the wall.

**ALI** *(sleepy, small)*
> مَامَا… هُوَّ بَابَا نَايِمْ دِلْوَقْتِي؟

**FATMA** *(soft, not moving)*
> نَامْ يَا عَلِي.

**ALI** *(he won't, yet)*
> هُوَّ بِيِنَامْ فِينْ؟

Fatma reaches up and straightens Sayed's photo, which is already straight.

**FATMA** *(a gentle lie, steady, for him)*
> فِي حِتَّةْ دَافْيَةْ… نَامْ بَقَى.

Ali closes his eyes. Fatma doesn't.

**SFX:** The muffled radio, indistinct; a car with taped headlights passing below.
**MUSIC:** March theme thins to a single sustained note under this beat, then returns with the march.

**Assets:** C-13 · C-14 · ENV-07 (night) · Sayed's photo

### Beat 2.8 — Into the dunes

**EXT. SAND DUNES, NORTHERN SINAI — NIGHT**

**Environment:** The ground rises. Soft dune sand, ankle-deep and shifting. Wind lifting fine grains off the crests.

The file climbs out of the marsh onto sand. Wet uniforms instantly coat with grit; mud and sand cake into a stiff crust. Sayed drops beside Mostafa and shows him on his own rifle: a loose cloth sleeve over the action, the trigger left free. He hands Mostafa a strip of rag.

**SAYED** *(whisper, working fast)*
> الرَّمْلَةْ دِي تِعَطَّلْ أَجْدَعْ سِلَاحْ. لِفُّهْ كِدَهْ… وِمَا تْفُكُّوشْ غِيرْ لَمَّا أَقُولَّكْ.

**MOSTAFA** *(whisper)*
> حَاضِرْ.

Close: sand sliding down a dune face over a boot. Men keep moving.

**SFX:** Sand hiss on the wind, heavy footsteps sinking into soft sand.
**MUSIC:** March theme continues, thinning out as the road comes into view.

**Assets:** ENV-01 · C-02 · C-03 · CMD-02 (sand-caked state) · CMD-08

---

### Beat 2.9 — The road and the wire

**EXT. ASPHALT ROAD → BARBED-WIRE BELT — NIGHT**

**Environment:** A ribbon of dark asphalt across the sand. Beyond it, a low fence of barbed wire on pickets.

The company crosses the road in silent bounds, two at a time. On the far side, the point man drops flat and raises a fist. Sayed crawls back to Shalabi.

**SAYED** *(whisper)*
> سِلْكْ شَايِكْ يَا فَنْدِمْ.

Shalabi crawls forward and looks at the wire. Wire beside a road means one thing.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper)*
> يِبْقَى وَرَاهْ أَلْغَامْ. هَاتْلِي حَسَنْ.

**SFX:** Boots on asphalt, quickly muffled; wind in the wire.
**MUSIC:** None — the score cuts out as the fist goes up.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · ENV-02 (first appearance — must match Beats 2.12–3.13) · barbed-wire prop

### Beat 2.10 — Breaching the minefield

**EXT. MINEFIELD — NIGHT**

**Environment:** Flat sand. Nothing visible. That is the danger.

**ENGINEER SERGEANT HASSAN** slides under the wire on his belly, probe in hand, a reel of white marking tape on his back. Before he goes, he looks back once at the file of men lying behind him.

**HASSAN** *(a whisper, slow, every word placed)*
> عَلَى الشَّرِيطْ وَبَسّْ. وَلَا شِبْرْ بَرَّاهْ.

He eases the probe into the sand at a shallow angle. Stops. A mine. He marks it, angles around it, and continues, paying out tape behind him.

Behind him, the entire company follows on their bellies along that single white line, weapons dragged beside them. A hand signal halts the file; 120 men freeze. Mostafa's elbow is an inch from the tape's edge. Adel, behind him, puts two fingers on Mostafa's boot — still. A second signal; the crawl resumes.

**SFX:** The probe sliding into sand. Breathing. Cloth dragging. Nothing else.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-03 · C-05 · C-06 · mine probe + tape reel props (CMD-12) · CMD-07 (Israeli mine variant — TBD)

### Beat 2.11 — Toward Romani

**EXT. FAR EDGE OF THE MINEFIELD — LATE NIGHT**

**Environment:** The faint grey of distant dune crests against the sky. Still dark.

The last man clears the tape. The company gathers in a fold of sand: mud to the waist, salt-white sleeves, sand in every crease, weapons still in hand. Shalabi counts them by touch, one shoulder at a time. Sayed comes in from the rear of the file.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper)*
> كَامْ؟

**SAYED** *(whisper)*
> كُلُّهُمْ يَا فَنْدِمْ. مَحَدِّشْ اتْأَذَى.

Shalabi lets out a breath he seems to have held since the wire. He checks the compass once more.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper, pointing east)*
> رُمَّانَةْ… مِنْ هِنَا.

They rise and move off into the dark.

**MUSIC:** The march theme returns quietly and carries into the next beat, fading as the company reaches the ambush area.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · CMD-02 (final wear state — carries into the ambush) · ENV-01

## SEQUENCE C — THE GROUND

### Beat 2.12 — A desert like a carpet

**EXT. AMBUSH AREA NEAR THE ROMANI ROAD — LATE NIGHT (OCT 6–7)**

**Environment:** Flat, open desert under starlight. No hills, no rocks, no bends in the road. The asphalt runs dead straight into the dark in both directions.

**VO**

> وَصَلَتِ السَّرِيَّةُ إِلَى مَنْطِقَةِ الْكَمِينِ فِي آخِرِ اللَّيْلِ.

Shalabi crawls to a low rise and looks. He turns his head slowly left, then right. Nothing to hide behind. Sayed crawls up beside him.

**SAYED** *(whisper, dismayed)*
> نِسْتَخَبَّى فِينْ هِنَا يَا فَنْدِمْ؟ دِي مَفْرُودَةْ زَيّْ السِّجَّادَةْ.

Shalabi scoops a handful of sand and lets it run through his fingers.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; whisper — the idea arriving as he says it)*
> يِبْقَى نِسْتَخَبَّى تَحْتْ السِّجَّادَةْ.

Behind them, the company lies flat in a long line, salt-crusted and sand-caked from the march.

**SFX:** Wind. A distant jackal call. Breathing.
**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · ENV-04 · ENV-02 · CMD-02 (sand-caked state)

### Beat 2.13 — Few against many

**EXT. AMBUSH AREA — LATE NIGHT — CONTINUOUS**

**Environment:** Same. Faces barely visible by starlight.

The company still lies together in one fold of ground, before the platoons spread out. Shalabi moves among the nearest men, crouching, looking each man in the face. Exhausted men straighten when he reaches them. He stops at the center and speaks just loud enough.

**SHALABI** *(low — dramatized)*
> يَا رِجَّالَةْ… هُمَّ كْتِيرْ، وِإِحْنَا قُلَيِّلِينْ. بَسّْ مَا يِعْرَفُوشْ إِحْنَا فِينْ.

Two or three of the men near him answer, a beat apart.

**COMMANDOS** *(two or three nearby men, a beat apart, murmuring)*
> تَمَامْ يَا فَنْدِمْ… إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهْ.

**SAYED** *(dry, quiet, to Mostafa beside him — a plain fact, not a speech)*
> اللِّوَا دَهْ لَوْ عَدَّى… يِبْقَى إِحْنَا كُلِّنَا رُحْنَا.

Mostafa, beside him, nods slowly.

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · C-03 · ensemble

### Beat 2.14 — Three platoons, three kilometers

**EXT. AMBUSH AREA — LATE NIGHT**

**Environment:** Same. A high, slow aerial view begins: the straight road, the flat desert, and small groups of men spreading out along it.

Shalabi kneels over his map with three platoon leaders, Sayed and Hassan, a red-filtered flashlight cupped in his hand. His finger marks three points along the road.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized)*
> فَصِيلَةْ هِنَا… كِيلُو… وِفَصِيلَةْ… كِيلُو… وِفَصِيلَةْ. وِكُلّْ فَصِيلَةْ تِسِيبْ جَمَاعَةْ وَرَا… تِحْمِي ضَهْرِنَا.

He looks up at Hassan.

**SHALABI** *(dramatized; low)*
> حَسَنْ… إِنْتَ وَرَا. مَحَدِّشْ يِيجِي مِنْ ضَهْرِنَا.

**HASSAN** *(slow, plain, no bravado)*
> يِعَدُّوا عَلَيَّا الْأَوِّلْ يَا فَنْدِمْ.

**AERIAL VIEW:** Three dark clusters move apart along the road, about a kilometer between each. From each cluster, a small group of 6–12 men peels away to the rear — one of them led by Hassan. On both flanks, Malutka teams, Adel among them, carry their suitcase launchers to positions overlooking the road.

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** C-01 · C-02 · C-05 · C-06 · CMD-10 · ENV-04 (deployment diagram) · ENV-02

### Beat 2.15 — The enemy who could not move

**EXT. THE ROMANI ROAD — LATE NIGHT**

**Environment:** The empty road, perfectly still. Starlight on the asphalt.

A long, static shot down the empty road. Nothing comes. In the foreground, out of focus, a commando's hand rests on an RPG tube.

**VO**

> كَانَ الْعَدُوُّ يَعْلَمُ أَنَّ سَرِيَّةَ صَاعِقَةٍ قَدْ نَزَلَتْ خَلْفَ خُطُوطِهِ… لَكِنَّهُ لَا يَعْلَمُ أَيْنَ. فَلَمْ يَتَحَرَّكِ اللِّوَاءُ فِي مَوْعِدِهِ… وَتَحَقَّقَتِ السَّاعَاتُ الثَّلَاثُ قَبْلَ أَنْ تُطْلَقَ رَصَاصَةٌ وَاحِدَةٌ.

**MUSIC:** None.

**Assets:** ENV-02 · CMD-06

### Beat 2.16 — Barrel pits

**EXT. AMBUSH AREA — NIGHT → PRE-DAWN**

**Environment:** Moonlight. Then the first cold grey in the east.

Intimate close-ups, as in the salt march:

- A short entrenching shovel slicing the top layer of sand away in one flat piece.
